First Ride

About Us

We pride ourselves on creating a premium-quality fitness experience that fosters community and personal transformation. At THE RIDE, community is everything! Our goal is for our fitness studio to be your happy place, in which you want to spend more time than you need to, where your workout becomes a treat, not a chore. THE RIDE community will motivate you to show up and give it your all, meeting new people, finding your crew! On a day when you are just not feeling it, they are there to motivate you, because finding your crew is easy when you have a common goal. We owe a huge thanks to our community for joining us on this awesome journey and we hope that you will continue to be a part of our story.

How It Works

Our schedule goes live 7 days in advance, and you may reserve your bikes for the coming week. Of course, you may reserve a bike at any time after that, up to 15 minutes prior to start time, based on availability. After that, we ask that you call the studio directly to ensure there is still space available. To book a bike, you must have paid rides in your account. The booking process will include choosing where in THE RIDE Theater you would like your bike to be positioned. There are three rows of bikes arranged facing forward, and we encourage all riders to choose a location most comfortable for them: whether for a first-time rider starting in the back of the class where bikes have the most space, or for a more advanced rider in the front row which may be the most intense workout. Remember, it is your RIDE!

Rules
  • Rules are made to be broken…except for these.
  • No cell phones in THE RIDE Theater. They disturb others and take away from your own experience. Unplugging can be very therapeutic: BE PRESENT.
  • Please be on time. To preserve the experience for other riders, we do not allow anyone to enter a class later than five minutes after the class has started. If you need to leave class early, please tell the instructor ahead of time, and wait until the last song, signaling to them on your way out that you are okay and not injured.
  • Respect the facility.
  • Have FUN!
Classes

For your first ride with us, we recommend arriving at least fifteen minutes before class. When you arrive at the studio, check in with our friendly front desk staff. You’ll need to sign a quick waiver, then you’ll be given a tour of the studio, and be assisted setting up on your bike. For subsequent rides, please note that if you have not arrived and signed in by class time, your bike may be released. If you are running late please just give the studio a call.
