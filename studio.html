It's a Full-Body Workout, Not Just Cardio
Classes combine free weights, core strengthening, stationary cycling, and creative choreography — meaning you get a complete workout in a single session rather than wandering between machines at a traditional gym.

The Ride delivers a cycling experience that could have been created only in Las Vegas — each class is as unique as any Las Vegas show, but you're part of the cast and burning calories the whole time. No generic treadmill sessions here.

Reviewers consistently describe The Ride not just as a cycling studio, but as a community built to lift people up to living their best life — with motivating instructors and an exhilarating atmosphere that keeps people coming back.
